### NOMBOR DAN OPERASI / NUMBERS AND OPERATIONS
1. \(a^m \times a^n = a^{m+n}\)
2. \(a^m \div a^n = a^{m-n}\)
3. \((a^m)^n = a^{mn}\)
4. \(a^{\frac{m}{n}} = (a^m)^{\frac{1}{n}} = (a^{\frac{1}{n}})^m = \sqrt[n]{a^m} = (\sqrt[n]{a})^m\)
5. Faedah mudah / *Simple interest*, \(I = Prt\)
6. Nilai matang / *Maturity value*, \(MV = P\left(1 + \frac{r}{n}\right)^{nt}\)
7. Jumlah bayaran balik / *Total repayment*, \(A = P + Prt\)
8. Premium asas insurans hayat / *Basic premium of life insurance*  
   \(= \frac{\text{Nilai muka polisi}}{\text{RM}R} \times (\text{Kadar premium setiap RM}R)\)  
   \(= \frac{\text{Face value of policy}}{\text{RM}R} \times (\text{Premium rate per RM}R)\)
9. Peruntukan ko-insurans / *Co-insurance provision*  
   \(\text{Bayaran pampasan} = \frac{\text{Jumlah insurans yang dibeli}}{\text{Jumlah insurans yang harus dibeli}} \times \text{Kerugian} - \text{Deduktibel}\)  
   \(\text{Amount of compensation} = \frac{\text{Amount of insurance purchased}}{\text{Amount of required insurance}} \times \text{Loss} - \text{Deductible}\)
10. Pendapatan bercukai / *Chargeable income*  
    \(= \text{Jumlah pendapatan tahunan} - \text{Pengecualian cukai} - \text{Pelepasan cukai}\)  
    \(= \text{Total annual income} - \text{Tax exemption} - \text{Tax relief}\)

### PERKAITAN DAN ALGEBRA / RELATIONSHIP AND ALGEBRA
1. Jarak / *Distance* \(= \sqrt{(x_2 - x_1)^2 + (y_2 - y_1)^2}\)
2. Titik tengah / *Midpoint*, \((x, y) = \left(\frac{x_1 + x_2}{2}, \frac{y_1 + y_2}{2}\right)\)
3. Laju purata / *Average speed* \(= \frac{\text{Jumlah jarak}}{\text{Jumlah masa}} / \frac{\text{Total distance}}{\text{Total time}}\)
4. Kecerunan / *Gradient*, \(m = \frac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1}\)
5. Kecerunan / *Gradient*, \(m = -\frac{\text{pintasan-}y}{\text{pintasan-}x} = -\frac{y\text{-intercept}}{x\text{-intercept}}\)
6. Matriks songsang / *Inverse matrix*,  
   \(A^{-1} = \frac{1}{ad - bc} \begin{pmatrix} d & -b \\ -c & a \end{pmatrix}\), di mana / *where* \(ad - bc \neq 0\)

### SUKATAN DAN GEOMETRI / MEASUREMENT AND GEOMETRY
1. Luas trapezium / *Area of trapezium* \(= \frac{1}{2} \times \text{hasil tambah sisi selari} \times \text{tinggi} = \frac{1}{2} \times \text{sum of parallel sides} \times \text{height}\)
2. Lilitan bulatan / *Circumference of circle* \(= 2\pi j = \pi d = 2\pi r\)
3. Luas bulatan / *Area of circle* \(= \pi j^2 = \pi r^2\)
4. Panjang lengkok / *Arc length* \(= \frac{\theta}{360^\circ} \times 2\pi j = \frac{\theta}{360^\circ} \times 2\pi r\)
5. Luas sektor / *Area of sector* \(= \frac{\theta}{360^\circ} \times \pi j^2 = \frac{\theta}{360^\circ} \times \pi r^2\)
6. Luas permukaan melengkung silinder / *Curved surface area of cylinder* \(= 2\pi j t = 2\pi r h\)
7. Luas permukaan melengkung kon / *Curved surface area of cone* \(= \pi j s = \pi r s\)
8. Luas permukaan sfera / *Surface area of sphere* \(= 4\pi j^2 = 4\pi r^2\)
9. Isipadu prisma tegak / *Volume of right prism* \(= \text{luas keratan rentas} \times \text{panjang} = \text{cross sectional area} \times \text{length}\)
10. Isipadu silinder / *Volume of cylinder* \(= \pi j^2 t = \pi r^2 h\)
11. Isipadu kon / *Volume of cone* \(= \frac{1}{3}\pi j^2 t = \frac{1}{3}\pi r^2 h\)
12. Isipadu sfera / *Volume of sphere* \(= \frac{4}{3}\pi j^3 = \frac{4}{3}\pi r^3\)
13. Isipadu piramid tegak / *Volume of right pyramid* \(= \frac{1}{3} \times \text{luas tapak} \times \text{tinggi} = \frac{1}{3} \times \text{base area} \times \text{height}\)
14. Hasil tambah sudut pedalaman poligon / *Sum of interior angles of a polygon* \(= (n - 2) \times 180^\circ\)
15. Faktor skala / *Scale factor*, \(k = \frac{PA'}{PA}\)
16. Luas imej / *Area of image* \(= k^2 \times \text{Luas objek} = k^2 \times \text{Area of object}\)

### STATISTIK DAN KEBARANGKALIAN / STATISTICS AND PROBABILITY
1. Min / *Mean*, \(\bar{x} = \frac{\sum x}{N}\) atau / *or* \(\bar{x} = \frac{\sum f x}{\sum f}\)
2. Varians / *Variance*, \(\sigma^2 = \frac{\sum (x - \bar{x})^2}{N} = \frac{\sum x^2}{N} - \bar{x}^2\) atau / *or* \(\sigma^2 = \frac{\sum f(x - \bar{x})^2}{\sum f} = \frac{\sum f x^2}{\sum f} - \bar{x}^2\)
3. Sisihan piawai / *Standard deviation*, \(\sigma = \sqrt{\sigma^2}\)
4. Kebarangkalian peristiwa / *Probability of an event*, \(P(A) = \frac{n(A)}{n(S)}\)
5. Kebarangkalian peristiwa pelengkap / *Complementary event*, \(P(A') = 1 - P(A)\)
6. Kebarangkalian peristiwa bergabung / *Probability of combined events*:
   - Peristiwa saling eksklusif / *Mutually exclusive*: \(P(A \cup B) = P(A) + P(B)\)
   - Peristiwa tidak saling eksklusif / *Non-mutually exclusive*: \(P(A \cup B) = P(A) + P(B) - P(A \cap B)\)
   - Peristiwa tidak bersandar / *Independent events*: \(P(A \cap B) = P(A) \times P(B)\)
