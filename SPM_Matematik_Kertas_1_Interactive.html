Q1
Ungkapkan $\frac{0.00048 \times 10^{-3}}{1.2 \times 10^5}$ dalam bentuk piawai.
Express $\frac{0.00048 \times 10^{-3}}{1.2 \times 10^5}$ in standard form.
A.
$4.0 \times 10^{-12}$
B.
$4.0 \times 10^{-11}$
C.
$4.0 \times 10^{-10}$
D.
$4.0 \times 10^{-9}$
[1 markah / 1 mark]
Marking Criteria
  • $0.00048 \times 10^{-3} = 4.8 \times 10^{-4} \times 10^{-3} = 4.8 \times 10^{-7}$ K1
  • $\frac{4.8 \times 10^{-7}}{1.2 \times 10^5} = 4.0 \times 10^{-7 - 5} = 4.0 \times 10^{-12}$ N1
Marker's Tip: Ensure indices of 10 are subtracted carefully: $-7 - 5 = -12$.

Sebuah graf mempunyai 6 bucu dengan darjah bucu 2, 3, 3, 4, $k$, 2. Diberi jumlah darjah graf ialah 16. Cari nilai $k$ dan bilangan tepi bagi graf itu.
A graph has 6 vertices with degrees 2, 3, 3, 4, $k$, 2. Given the sum of degrees of the graph is 16. Find the value of $k$ and the number of edges of the graph.

$k = 2$, Number of edges = 8
B.
$k = 2$, Bilangan tepi = 16
$k = 2$, Number of edges = 16

$x = 3, x = -2$
B.
$x = -3, x = 2$
C.
$x = 4, x = -3$
D.
$x = 6, x = -1$
[1 markah / 1 mark]
